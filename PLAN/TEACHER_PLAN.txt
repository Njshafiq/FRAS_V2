### 🔒 Locked Attendance — Final Rule

```text
Attendance Submitted
        ↓
Attendance Locked
        ↓
Teacher finds an error
        ↓
Send Correction Request
        ↓
Admin Reviews
        ↓
Admin Approves
        ↓
Attendance Modified
        ↓
Audit Log
```

**Teacher cannot directly modify locked attendance.**
